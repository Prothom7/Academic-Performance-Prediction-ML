# Reference Paper

`academic_performance_paper.pdf` is the included Data in Brief article:

**Dataset of academic performance evolution for engineering students**  
Enrique Delahoz-Dominguez, Rohemi Zuluaga, Tomas Fontalvo-Herrera  
Data in Brief 30 (2020) 105537.

The article describes a Colombian engineering-student dataset with 12,411 observations and 44 variables, combining secondary-education and professional standardized-test information with social/context variables. It is primarily a **data article**, not a paper reporting the same RandomForest benchmark used in this project. Therefore any final “base paper vs our model” comparison should distinguish dataset/methodology comparison from accuracy comparison unless an additional predictive study is supplied.
