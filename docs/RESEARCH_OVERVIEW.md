# Research overview

This project evaluates attainment, academic mobility and research engagement in the current full analytical population, retaining all 3516 records and 32 original variables. The conceptual proposition is assessed through the [master evidence table](../results/tables/master_evidence_table.csv) and [verified conclusions](../results/tables/verified_conclusions.csv), rather than assumed.

## Research questions

1. How do academic-performance distributions change between Year 1 and Year 2?
2. How strongly does first-year academic standing persist into the second year?
3. Which pre-university, socioeconomic, educational and institutional characteristics are associated with academic standing?
4. Which characteristics distinguish improved, stable and declined academic standing?
5. How do associations vary across gender, faculty, institution, college background and living arrangement?
6. Which academic, institutional, socioeconomic and extracurricular characteristics are associated with research participation?
7. How much additional predictive information does Year-1 standing provide for Year-2 standing beyond background variables?
8. How similar are the association profiles of attainment, mobility and research engagement?

Study A reproduces frozen five-class Year-1 prediction. Study B reproduces the original WEKA research experiment, its Python approximation, and a current-population Python experiment. Study C compares Year-2 prediction without and with Year-1 standing on identical rows and a common configuration. Descriptive and adjusted association analyses address the scientific questions beyond predictive metrics.
